# Why AceMath

### What is the Background that lead to AceMath solution?

- Many science learners who do grade 10 to 12 Mathematics in secondary school are not well trained to be __problem solvers__.
- This is because much of the focus is on covering the curriculum so that when the exam period comes the necessary topics are covered.
- Even after the curriculum has been covered, teachers do not focus on training the learners to solve different types of problems that they can be tested on, instead they give them past question papers with which to practice, but may not be effective especially when the learners have no idea how to solve those problems.
- Therefore, learners are not really trained to be problem solvers, because if that were so, then the emphasis would be on training them on how to identify different types of problems and then solve.

---

### What is the objective of AceMath?

- This solution aims to teach the learners the minimal but necessary theory that will enable them to __identify__ different types of Mathematics problems, and show how to __solve__ them.
- This solution focuses on training learners to be __problem solvers__, as a result, the majority of its content are problems and their corresponding solutions.
